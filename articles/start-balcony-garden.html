How to Start a Balcony Garden in India

A step-by-step guide for Indian apartment beginners.

Balcony gardening is a practical way to bring greenery into an Indian apartment. Before selecting plants, consider your balcony’s available light, container space and local growing season.

Choose your space

Measure the usable space and consider wind, drainage and sunlight.

Pick suitable plants

Match plants to the amount of direct sun your balcony gets.

Prepare pots and soil

Choose drainage-friendly containers and an appropriate potting mix.

Water and maintain

Check soil moisture, feed plants as needed and watch for pests.

Final Tip

Start with a few suitable plants, observe how they respond to your balcony conditions and adjust your care routine as you learn.
