Vegetables You Can Grow on an Indian Balcony

Grow fresh vegetables in pots and containers.

Balcony gardening is a practical way to bring greenery into an Indian apartment. Before selecting plants, consider your balcony’s available light, container space and local growing season.

Tomatoes

Choose compact varieties, provide strong sunlight and use a roomy container.

Green Chillies

Grow in warm conditions with several hours of direct sun.

Spinach

Plant during a suitable cooler season and harvest tender leaves.

Radishes

Use a container deep enough for the roots and keep soil evenly moist.

Final Tip

Start with a few suitable plants, observe how they respond to your balcony conditions and adjust your care routine as you learn.
