Best Plants for Low-Sunlight Apartment Balconies

Great greenery for balconies with less direct sun.

Balcony gardening is a practical way to bring greenery into an Indian apartment. Before selecting plants, consider your balcony’s available light, container space and local growing season.

Snake Plant

Can tolerate low light, although growth is often slower.

Money Plant

Prefers bright indirect light and tolerates moderate shade.

Peace Lily

Likes filtered light and protection from intense direct sun.

ZZ Plant

Handles lower-light spaces and needs careful, infrequent watering.

Final Tip

Start with a few suitable plants, observe how they respond to your balcony conditions and adjust your care routine as you learn.
